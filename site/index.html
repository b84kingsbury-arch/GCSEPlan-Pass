GCSE Plan & Pass

Plan your week. Pass your GCSEs.

Week and revision blocks

Set each day's start and end time, how many blocks and how long. Block times are worked out for you, at least 15 minutes apart and around your commitments.

Commitments

Meal times, sports practice, clubs, seeing friends, part-time work. Revision blocks move to fit around them; if there isn't room, the fewest blocks needed are removed.

Subjects and priority

High priority subjects get twice as many blocks as low priority ones. Medium sits in between.

Subjects changed. Rebuild to update the plan.

Worksheets are written with AI in the style of each exam board's papers and checked by teachers. They are not past papers. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by any exam board.
